Privacy

This site doesn’t use ads, analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies, and it doesn’t ask for any personal information.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of each visitor for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account (GitHub, n.d.). We can’t see or turn off these records. (Unchecked by a person; AI research only.)

Fonts and images. The fonts and images on this site are stored on the site itself rather than loaded from services such as Google Fonts, so your browser doesn’t contact those companies when you visit.

Feedback. When we add a feedback form, we’ll say here what it collects and who can see it.
