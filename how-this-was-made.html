How This Was Made

In this section, text in purple was written primarily by a human; the rest was written primarily by Claude, relying on human input. (If you can’t see the different colors, see the distinction a different way.)

This site was planned and drafted by engaging a large language model, Claude, by Anthropic, used in the Claude desktop app (Cowork). A human wrote a detailed project plan (about 3,000 words), which included naming concerns with AI and attempting to encourage Claude to incorporate a critical approach in its output. Claude was prompted, in an interactive process with human feedback, to draft plans, research summaries, page text, code, and the version 1 brain drawings. The website will go no further until and unless a team of humans, with various kinds of expertise and perspectives, decides it is worth pursuing, including checking science claims.

For AI usage and the limits of AI, see Wrestling with AI. Chat excerpts and the project log may be added here.
